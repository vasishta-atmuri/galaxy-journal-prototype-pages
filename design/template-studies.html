Galaxy Journal / Design study

Four ways into a page.

Try the controls. These are visual concepts; nothing is saved or sent to AI.

Open your journal ↗
1

Photo with meaning

PHOTO MEMORY

More than
a photograph.

Keep the part the camera missed.

What would this photo not tell you later?
Illustrative response, not live AIWith a caption about an unexpectedly quiet afternoon: “Was the quiet what made it special, or was it who you were with?”

Start here. A real photo anchors recall. One caption adds what the image cannot capture. No automatic location or feeling is assumed.

2

A day in three moments

MY DAY

Not the whole day.
Just what stayed.

One moment is enough. The others can wait.

More than one can be true. Or leave it open.

Illustrative response, not live AIIf you describe a busy day but choose “light”: “What kept the day feeling light, even with so much happening?”

Build next. Three optional cards give shape to scattered thoughts. Later, permitted context could suggest a moment for you to accept or replace.

3

Before & after movement

MOVEMENT & REST

How it felt,
not just what you did.

A little movement. A full workout. A rest day. All belong here.

Optional. No rating is chosen until you move a slider.

Illustrative response, not live AIIf you report choosing rest and feeling better: “Did taking the rest feel easy to allow yourself, or was there a bit of guilt too?”

Test the empathy agent here. An optional before/after comparison reveals a difference worth exploring. Missing logs must never be treated as missed exercise.

4

A new chapter

NEW CHAPTER
A page to return to

Some things change.
Some come with you.

A move, a role, a relationship—or a shift only you can name.

What feels different

What still feels like you

No need to make this a fresh start.

Illustrative response, not live AIIf this page mentions unfamiliar routines and an old friendship: “Has that friendship helped things feel more familiar, or does it feel separate from this change?”

Add after the first three. Two contrasting spaces make room for mixed feelings. Returning later could reveal change through the user’s own words.

Recommendation: implement Photo memory, Three moments and Before & after first. Keep New chapter as the continuity study. Visual structure should reduce effort; every field is optional, and free writing remains available. These are reusable capture formats within the same journal. Example follow-ups illustrate intent and are not hardcoded product rules.
